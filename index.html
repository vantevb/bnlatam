How it works

Every message is read by our team, translated into Thai and placed under your country.

  1. CreateWrite a letter, make a drawing or record a short video for Boss and Noeul.
  2. SendUpload it through the form, with your name and country.
  3. TranslateWe translate every message into Thai and publish it here.
  4. ShowIn the video call, we will tell Boss and Noeul the site exists and show it to them on screen. Visiting it and reading the messages is entirely up to them. They have no obligation at all.27·12

Questions

Do I need a Google account?

Only to attach a drawing or a video, because Google stores the files. A letter can be sent the same way.

Why do videos need subtitles?

So we can translate what you say into Thai. Without subtitles, we cannot guarantee Boss and Noeul will understand your message.

Which language can I write in?

Any. Portuguese, Spanish, English, or even Thai. We take care of the translation.

Who sees my message?

Everyone who visits this site, and Boss and Noeul. Your email is never published. Only the name you choose and your country appear.

Is this official?

No. BN LATAM is an independent fan project by Entre BLs & Desejos, made with love and without any connection to the artists' companies.

Will Boss and Noeul read my message?

In the video call, we will tell them the site exists and show it on screen. Whether they visit it and read the messages is entirely up to them. They have no obligation at all.
